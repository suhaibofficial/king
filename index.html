This is the official website of SB, created as the central home for web design, digital experiences, creative development, and visual work. It brings together the different areas of creativity and technology that define SB, with a focus on thoughtful design, strong visual direction, intuitive experiences, and carefully developed digital work.

SB is focused on designing and building for the web. This includes website design, interface design, front-end development, interaction, animation, creative technology, and other forms of digital creation. The work combines design and technology to create experiences that are visually distinctive while remaining clear, responsive, functional, and enjoyable to use.

Design is at the centre of everything created through SB. Typography, composition, spacing, movement, colour, imagery, interaction, and structure are considered together rather than as separate elements. The intention is to create digital experiences that feel natural and purposeful, where visual quality and usability support each other instead of competing for attention.

Development is equally important to the way SB works. Websites and digital experiences are built with attention to responsiveness, performance, accessibility, interaction, and technical detail. The aim is to make technology feel natural within the experience, allowing the design to remain clear while the underlying systems provide the functionality required to make everything work smoothly.

SB also explores digital art, visual design, motion, creative coding, photography, music, wallpapers, videos, and other forms of digital creativity. These areas provide additional ways to explore ideas, visual language, technology, and expression while remaining connected to the wider focus on design and digital experiences.

The approach behind SB is based on continuous refinement. Design decisions are questioned, details are adjusted, and ideas are developed through observation, learning, testing, and iteration. Simplicity, clarity, curiosity, precision, and attention to detail remain important throughout this process.

Every reasonable effort is made to keep the information presented throughout this website accurate, relevant, and up to date. However, information, external resources, recommendations, technical details, availability, compatibility, and other website content may change over time. The experience of using certain features may also vary depending on the device, browser, operating system, software, screen size, and internet connection being used.

This website serves as the official digital home of SB, providing a place to discover what SB creates, how design and technology come together, and the different forms of digital work associated with the name. It is intended to remain a clear and evolving representation of SB and its continued focus on creating thoughtful, distinctive, and well-crafted experiences for the web.
